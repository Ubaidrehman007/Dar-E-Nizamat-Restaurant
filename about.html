About Us

Welcome to Royal Spice Restaurant, where every meal is a celebration of taste and tradition.
Our Story

Royal Spice Restaurant was founded in 2010 with a simple goal — to serve delicious, hygienic and quality food to our guests.

From a small family kitchen to a favorite dining spot, our journey has been built on passion, dedication and love for cooking.

Today, we are proud to serve thousands of happy customers every month.
Our Mission

  • To provide high quality and fresh food.
  • To maintain hygiene and cleanliness.
  • To deliver excellent customer service.
  • To create a warm and welcoming atmosphere.
 		| Our Vision

  • To become the most loved restaurant brand.
  • To expand our services while maintaining quality.
  • To innovate new dishes for our customers.
  • To contribute to a healthier and happier society.
Our Chef

Our Head Chef, Chef Arjun Verma, has more than 15 years of experience in the culinary world.

He specializes in Indian, Chinese and Continental cuisines.

His creativity and passion bring unique flavors to every dish.
