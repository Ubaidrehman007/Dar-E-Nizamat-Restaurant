The Royal Heritage Of Dar E Nizamat

Located opposite Palm Riviera in Mumbra, Dar E Nizamat (दर निज़ामात) was created to bring authentic Mughlai feast culture and unmatched ambiance together under one roof.

Renowned for our signature rich chicken gravies and melt-in-mouth mutton cuts, we invite families and food enthusiasts to indulge in a luxurious culinary escape without the exorbitant pricing.
